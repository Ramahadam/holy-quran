Make space for daily Quran reading with Holy Quran.

TWO READING EXPERIENCES
Choose Classic mode for flowing Arabic text, or Mushaf mode for a page-faithful reading experience across the 604 Quran pages. Browse by Surah or Juz and switch between the two modes.

KEEP YOUR PLACE
Save ayahs and return to your last-read position. Bookmarks and reading progress are stored locally on your device. Create an encrypted backup to keep a copy of your saved reading state.

READ AT YOUR OWN PACE
Use light or dark appearance and navigate the app in English or Arabic. Bundled Quran content is available for offline reading after installation.

EXPLORE TAFSIR
Open ayah details and request Tafsir from available sources. Tafsir requests need an internet connection.

PRAYER READING REMINDERS
Set a reading reminder around a chosen prayer time, with an offset that fits your routine. Notifications depend on the permissions and scheduling access available on your device.

SHARE FEEDBACK
Send an anonymous note to help improve the reading experience. Feedback needs an internet connection; do not include personal or private information in your message.

Holy Quran is a focused reading companion for returning to the Quran each day.
